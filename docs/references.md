## Aircraft Parameter

Beard, R.W., and McLain, T.W.
Small Unmanned Aircraft: Theory and Practice.

Used for:
- Geometry: Mass, Wingspan, Wing Area
- Mass Properties: Mass, Ixx, Iyy, Izz, Ixz
- Baseline Aero Coeffs: CL0, CD0, CD_P, CY0, Cm0, Cl0, Cn0
- Stability Derivatives (Longitudinal): CL_alpha, CD_alpha, Cm_alpha, CL_q, CD_q, Cm_q
- Stability Derivatives (lateral/Directional): CY_beta, Cl_beta, Cn_beta, CY_p, Cl_p, Cn_p, CY_r, Cl_r, Cn_r
- Control Derivatives: CL_delta_e, CD_delta_e, Cm_delta_e, CY_delta_a, Cl_delta_a, Cn_delta_a, CY_delta_r, Cl_delta_r,  Cn_delta_r
- Propulsion Parameters: P_max, V_max, D_prop, K_V, K_Q, R_motor, i0, CQ_2, CQ_1, CQ_0, CT_2, CT_1, CT_0
